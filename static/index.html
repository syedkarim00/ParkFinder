Ontario Parks availability helper

Find Friday–Sunday campsites with a waterfront bias.

ParkFinder checks Ontario Parks reservation API availability for every complete Friday-to-Sunday weekend in your summer range, decodes campsite attributes, and ranks sites that appear adjacent to lakes, rivers, shorelines, beaches, or other bodies of water.
